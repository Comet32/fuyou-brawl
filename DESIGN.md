# Design System: 福佑大乱斗 · 图鉴与攻略

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Draft Desk"**

The site is the broadcast booth during a Captain's-Mode draft: a stage black-blue ground, slate panel bars laid on it, the game's three quality colors painted as solid team bars, and one broadcast gold that means "this one" (focus, selection, current, locked). Every blessing is a pick slot; the catalog is the draft pool, the compare page is the draft board, and the detail page is a lower-third nameplate. The system is dark by default, with a light "daytime booth" translation of the same tokens that the visitor opts into.

Density is operator-grade and phone-first. The first viewport on a 375px screen stacks a 48px scoreboard top bar, a 56px auto-focused lookup bar, one row of four team bars with tallies, and a scrolling chip strip, and results begin right after. Cards are compact (92px minimum), numerals are condensed and tabular, and nothing moves unless it tells you something: a result reorders, a pick locks, a sheet rises.

Color is flat and fully committed. There are no gradients, no glass and no glow anywhere in the build. Depth comes from bars overlapping bars (the nameplate over its quality band, the tally block cut into its team bar), with a soft ambient shadow reserved for things that float over the desk. Unknown data has its own material, a hatched hollow, and it is never faked with a gray that could be read as a fourth quality.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Stage black-blue ground, three steps of slate panel, one hairline line color.
- Quality colors (orange / purple / blue) are information, painted as solid fills; each has a text-safe ink variant.
- One broadcast gold for focus, selection, the current page, and the locked state.
- Rounded desk: 14px cards, 12px tiles, 10px controls, pill chips; a 12° slant survives only as a small leading cap.
- Condensed tabular numerals (Barlow Condensed) on every count, tally and value.
- Hatched hollow for anything unknown or unlabeled.
- Motion is expo-out and short (160–260ms), and all of it collapses under reduced motion.

## Colors

A near-black blue stage with slate panels, three game-defined team colors, and a single gold, all flat.

### Primary
- **Broadcast Gold** (`gold`): the one "this one" color. It is used for the focus ring (through `gold-ink`), the lookup cap while focused, the input caret, selected chips, the current-tab underline and dock notch, the lock outline and 已锁定 stamp, the hero tile's flag and hover strap, the sheet grabber, text selection, and link underlines. It never marks quality.
- **Gold Ink** (`gold-ink`): gold used as text or outline. In dark mode it is the same value as `gold`. In light mode it deepens to a readable brown-gold, so any gold text or focus ring must use this token, not `gold`.
- **On Gold** (`on-gold`): stage-dark ink for text on gold fills.

### Secondary (quality team colors, game-defined)
- **Legend Orange** (`q-ssr`): 橙色 (ssr) quality. Used for team-bar tallies, group heads, icon plinths, lower-third bands, the compare lock wipe and label-desk answers. Text on it uses stage ink.
- **Epic Purple** (`q-sr`): 紫色 (sr) quality, in the same roles. Text on it uses `on-sr` (white); it is the only quality with light on-fill ink.
- **Rare Blue** (`q-r`): 蓝色 (r) quality, in the same roles. Text on it uses stage ink.
- **Quality Inks** (`q-ssr-ink`, `q-sr-ink`, `q-r-ink`): lifted versions of each hue for small text on panels (the quality word in a card's meta line, suggestion rows). In light mode they deepen for AA contrast.

### Tertiary (description template tones and attribute grounds)
- **Value Green** (`hl`): highlighted terms and known numeric values in blessing description templates.
- **Callout Yellow** (`accent-ink`): accented template words.
- **Warn Red** (`warn-ink`): error status text on the label desk. This is the only error color.
- **Attribute Grounds** (`attr-str`, `attr-agi`, `attr-int`, `attr-all`): muted tints behind hero portraits while they load or when they fail, keyed by the hero's primary attribute. They are loading grounds only and are never used for UI chrome.

### Neutral
- **Stage** (`stage`): page ground, the scrollbar track, the sheet body, and the inverse tally block inside a group head.
- **Hollow** (`hollow`): below-stage depth for empty picks, empty states, textareas and the timeline track. It reads as "a slot with nothing in it".
- **Panel** (`panel`): the default bar and card fill, plus the top bar, dock, lookup field and lower-third.
- **Panel 2** (`panel-2`): hover step for panels, the default button fill, and the hero tile ground.
- **Panel 3** (`panel-3`): pressed or current step, section straps, tags, the lookup cap at rest, and icon fallbacks.
- **Line** (`line`): every 1px rule (the top-bar and dock edges, table rows, list dividers, hollow borders) and the lookup field's resting underline.
- **Ink / Ink 2 / Ink 3** (`ink`, `ink-2`, `ink-3`): primary text, secondary text (ledes, summaries, inactive tabs), and tertiary text (meta, placeholders, inactive dock items).
- **Hatch** (`hatch`): stroke color of the hatched-hollow material and its 1px border.

The light theme (`:root[data-theme='light']`) redefines the same token names. It is not a separate palette. Its values are recorded in `.impeccable/design.json` under `colorMeta.*.light`.

### Named Rules
**The Team Color Is Information Rule.** Orange, purple and blue mean ssr, sr and r, and nothing else. They are applied through `data-q` (which sets `--q`, `--qt`, `--on-q`), always as solid fills or their ink variants, and never as decoration, borders on unrelated UI, or hover tints.

**The One Gold Rule.** Gold marks the one thing currently chosen or focused. If two unrelated golds are visible in the same region, one of them is wrong.

**The Hatched Hollow Rule.** Unknown quality or an unknown value is shown as a transparent body with a 1px `hatch` border and a masked diagonal hatch (6px tile, 1.4px stroke). It is never a gray fill, never a fourth color, and never a guessed number.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (latin, weights 500–800, self-hosted from @fontsource), falling back to Fuyou Display, a self-hosted Noto Sans SC Black subset at weight 900, then PingFang SC / Microsoft YaHei.
**Body Font:** PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei, Noto Sans CJK SC, system-ui.
**Numeral Font:** Barlow Condensed, tabular.
**Code Font:** ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas.

**Character:** A condensed scoreboard grotesk for Latin and numbers over a heavy CJK sans, which gives broadcast-graphics authority with no reading cost. Fixed chrome strings use the Black subset, and all dynamic text stays on the system CJK font.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, 28px, 1.25): page titles (三选一对比, 英雄搭配…). The prose-page h1 is 32px.
- **Nameplate** (800, 30px mobile / 44px from 721px, 1.15): the blessing or hero name on the lower-third. The English name sits under it at Barlow 600 18px, uppercase, 0.03em tracking.
- **Headline** (900, 19px, 1.25): quality group heads (display face). Plain h2 headings are 800 19px.
- **Strap** (800, 17px): section straps on detail pages (完整效果, 数值表…), in the display face.
- **Title** (800, 17px, 1.3): card names in result slots. On compare cards they are 20px, and 17px on phones.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.6): the base size. Card summaries run 14.5px/1.6; the full effect and prose run 17px/1.85, capped at 70ch.
- **Label** (600–900, 12–15px): chips (14px/600), team bars (14–15px/900), tabs (15px/700), dock (12px/700), all in the display face.
- **Meta** (13px, 1.4, ink-3): the card meta line, tip meta, and captions.
- **Numerals** (Barlow Condensed 700–800, tabular): score 30px, group tally 26px, stats value 22px, team tally 19px, pick number 30px, guide index 28px, 404 at 96px.

### Named Rules
**The Scoreboard Numeral Rule.** Every count, tally, value and index is set in Barlow Condensed with `tabular-nums` (`.num` or `--font-num`), so the numbers align on one baseline grid. The body also sets `tabular-nums` globally.

**The Closed Glyph Set Rule.** Fuyou Display renders only the fixed chrome strings listed in `src/lib/display-glyphs.ts`. A new chrome string goes into that list and then `npm run fonts` is run. Blessing names, hero names and descriptions never use the display face.

## Layout

The layout is a single centered column with a 1240px maximum width and a 16px gutter (24px from 721px). The top bar is sticky at 48px (56px from 721px), and the lookup bar sticks directly under it on the stage ground, bleeding out to the gutters. On phones a fixed bottom dock (60px plus the safe-area inset) holds 图鉴 / 对比 / 英雄 / 更多. From 721px the dock is hidden and the same destinations become top broadcast tabs.

- **Breakpoints (observed):** 480px (team bars widen), 560px (the hero grid goes to auto-fill 176px), 721px (desktop chrome, 2-column results, lower-third enlarges), 900px (3-column draft board), 1000px (detail two-column grid, 1.3fr / 1fr), 1080px (3-column results).
- **Catalog:** with no query, results are grouped into four quality families (橙色 / 紫色 / 蓝色 / 未标注), each headed by a 48px group-head bar with its tally. With a query, one flat result list replaces the groups.
- **Filters:** four team bars in a `3 × 1fr + 1.35fr` grid with a 5px gap (the 未标注 bar is wider), then the tag strip. The strip scrolls horizontally on phones and wraps from 721px.
- **Rhythm:** an 8px gap between cards, chips and list items; 16px between groups; 36px above each section strap; 44px minimum touch targets (icon buttons 48 × 44).
- **Measure:** prose, effects, notes and tips are capped at 70ch; the footer at 75ch.
- **Performance-bound:** result `li`s use `content-visibility: auto` with a 124px intrinsic size, because the catalog renders every blessing on one page.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are flat and tonal. Depth is primarily overlap: the lower-third nameplate sits over its quality band, the tally block is cut into its team bar, and the icon stands on a quality plinth. The panel ramp (stage, panel, panel-2, panel-3) and the below-stage `hollow` carry the rest. Shadows are soft, dark and ambient, never colored and never glowing. They appear only on elements that float above the desk.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`--shadow`: `0 12px 28px -10px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): a locked compare card (together with `translate: 0 -4px`) and the suggestion popover.
- **Lift Small** (`--shadow-sm`: `0 4px 12px -4px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`): the lower-third quality band under the nameplate.
- **Dock Edge** (`0 -6px 18px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5)`): the fixed bottom dock.
- **Sheet** (`0 -16px 40px -12px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7)`): the 更多 bottom sheet, over a `rgb(5 7 11 / 0.6)` backdrop.
- The light theme retints Lift and Lift Small to `rgb(20 32 56 / …)`.

### Named Rules
**The Filled Slot Lifts Rule.** An empty slot is a dark hollow sunk below the stage. A filled, locked slot rises 4px and casts the Lift shadow. Elevation expresses state, not decoration.

**The No Glow Rule.** No gradients, no `backdrop-filter`, no `text-shadow`, and no colored shadows. Emphasis comes from a solid fill or gold, never from light.

## Shapes

The desk is rounded, a decision the owner pinned on 2026-09-29, with a strict radius ladder: **cards 14px** (result slots, compare cards, lower-third, guide rows, empty states, answer buttons), **tiles 12px** (hero tiles, group heads, pick heads, suggestion popover, the icon plinth, the 更多 list), **controls 10px** (buttons, tabs, lookup field, team bars, textarea, item chips, the score block), **straps 8px** (section straps, icon images inside plinths), **thumbs 6px** (suggestion and item thumbnails), **hairline 4px** (inline unknown-value marks), and **pill** for chips, tags, switches, stamps, hero chips and counters. The dock and sheet round only their top corners (16px).

Anything anchored to an edge rounds only its free corners. Name straps and ability straps on hero tiles are `0 8px 8px 0`, the hero flag is `0 0 0 10px`, the lower-third band is `0 0 14px 14px`, and the current-tab underline and dock notch round only their inner side.

Every bar fill lives on `::before`, radiused by `--r`, so focus outlines on the element itself stay visible and are never clipped.

### Named Rules
**The Last Slant Rule.** The 12° slant (offset = height × 0.2126) survives in exactly two places: the trailing edge of the lookup field's search cap (12px on the 56px field, 10px on the 48px compare field), and the leading edge of tally blocks (8.5px in team bars, 10.2px in group heads). It never appears on a card, tile, button or chip silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid and quiet, with gold reserved for the one primary action.
- **Shape:** control corners (10px), 44px minimum height, 15px/700 label.
- **Default:** a `panel-2` fill with `ink` text. Hover steps to `panel-3`; active nudges down 1px.
- **Gold:** a `gold` fill with `on-gold` text. Hover brightens it (`brightness(1.08)`).
- **Disabled:** 0.45 opacity and a not-allowed cursor, with no nudge.
- **Icon button:** 48 × 44, transparent, `ink-2`. Hover shows `panel-2`, active `panel-3`.
- **Link button:** gold-ink text, 800 weight, underlined.

### Chips
- **Style:** pill, 34px tall, a `panel` fill, `ink-2` 14px/600 text in the display face.
- **State:** hover steps to `panel-2` with `ink`. Pressed (`aria-pressed=true`) is a solid `gold` fill with `on-gold` 800 text.
- **Switch variant** (含英雄福佑): the same pill with a 30 × 18 knob. When on, the knob fills gold and its thumb slides 12px (160ms expo).
- **Tag:** a smaller pill (30px) on `panel-3`. Tags that are links turn gold on hover.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** card (14px).
- **Background:** `panel`, stepping to `panel-2` on hover. Empty containers use `hollow` with a 1px `line` border.
- **Shadow Strategy:** flat at rest (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none on filled cards. Focus-within draws a 2px `gold-ink` outline inset by 2px on the card itself.
- **Internal Padding:** result slot `10px 14px 12px 10px`, compare card 16px, lower-third `20px 20px 22px` (`28px 32px 30px` from 721px).
- **Whole-card link:** the name link's `::after` covers the card, and the name turns gold-ink on hover.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** the lookup field is a 56px control-radius bar on `panel` (48px on compare), with an 18px/600 input, a 2px `line` underline, and a leading search cap on `panel-3` cut with the last slant.
- **Focus:** the underline and the whole cap turn gold (cap text becomes `on-gold`), and the caret is `gold-ink`. The browser's native search cancel is removed; a 52px clear icon button appears only when the field has text.
- **Textarea** (label desk): 10px radius, a `hollow` fill, a 1px `line` border, 13px mono text.
- **Error:** `warn-ink` status text. There is no red field chrome.

### Navigation
- **Top bar:** a sticky `panel` bar with a 1px `line` bottom edge. It holds the three-stripe quality mark and 福佑图鉴 (18px/900), and at the trailing end the score block (a `panel-3` control with a 30px/800 Barlow count) and the theme toggle.
- **Tabs (721px and up):** transparent control-radius bars with 15px/700 `ink-2` text. Hover shows a `panel-2` fill; current shows a `panel-3` fill with a 3px gold underline.
- **Dock (phones):** a fixed `panel` bar with 16px top corners, four columns, each a 22px icon over a 12px/700 label in `ink-3`. Current turns `ink` with a 34 × 4 gold notch at the top edge. 更多 opens a bottom sheet (popover, 200ms rise) where the popover API is supported, and falls back to the /more/ page otherwise.
- **Icons:** one authored set on a 24px grid with a 2px stroke, square caps and miter joins (`src/components/Icon.astro`), sized 20px by default and 22px in the dock and lookup cap.

### Quality Team Bar (signature)
A 40px control-radius bar showing the quality word in 900 weight, with a solid tally block of the team color cut in by the last slant at its trailing end (Barlow 700 19px, `on-q` ink). When pressed, the whole bar fills with its team color and the label gains a 3px gold underline. The 未标注 bar's tally is the hatched hollow.

### Result Slot and Quality Plinth (signature)
A card with a 64px icon column. The 56px icon (8px corners) stands on a 12px-radius plinth of solid team color with a 16px foot, so quality reads from across the room. When quality is unknown there is no plinth: a 6px hatched bar sits under the icon instead. The meta line leads with the quality word in its quality ink.

### Group Head
A 48px, 12px-radius bar filled solid with the family's team color, carrying the family name (19px/900, `on-q`) and, at the trailing end, an inverse `stage` tally block (Barlow 800 26px) with the slant cap. The 未标注 family is transparent with a 1px hatch border and a hatched tally. The hero page reuses it at 40px.

### Lower-Third (signature)
The detail nameplate: a `panel` card with an 88px icon plinth (128px from 721px) and the name, overlapping a full-width 60px band of solid quality color (72px from 721px), inset 20px (48px from 721px) and rounded only at the bottom. The band carries the quality word right-aligned and a small source caption at its leading end. When quality is unknown, the band is the hatched hollow with its label on a `stage` pill.

### Draft Board and Lock-In (signature)
Three pick columns, each with a 44px pick head (pick number in Barlow 800 30px, then 待选 or the 已锁定 gold pill stamp), a 48px lookup field, and a card. An empty card is a hollow with a centered hint. Locking wipes the head in the pick's quality color from left to right (`clip-path` inset, 220ms `--ease-expo`), stamps 已锁定 (260ms, scale 1.35 to 1), rings the head with a 2px gold outline offset 2px, and lifts the card 4px with the Lift shadow. An unlabeled lock uses the hatched hollow, not a color.

### Unknown Value Mark
An inline hatched hollow with a hairline radius (4px) and a 1px hatch border, showing `ink-3` Barlow text; in the stats table it widens to a minimum of 5em. It stands in for any `{placeholder}` without a known number.

### Section Strap
An inline-block `panel-3` strap with 8px corners, set in the display face at 17px, followed by an optional `ink-3` count. It heads each section on the detail and hero pages.

### Motion
One easing, `--ease-expo` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Results FLIP-slide 160ms while typing, and newly revealed slots rise from 14px below at 0.35 opacity. The lock wipe runs 220ms, the stamp 260ms, the sheet rise 200ms, the switch knob 160ms and the label timeline fill 200ms. Page navigation uses cross-document view transitions: the top bar and dock hold still while the content crossfades in 200ms, and going into a blessing or hero detail page the tapped card's icon (or portrait) and name morph into the header in 240ms, then back again on return. Browsers without support simply navigate. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` every animation and transition collapses to 1ms, FLIP is skipped, and page transitions are off.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** apply quality through `data-q` so `--q`, `--qt` and `--on-q` resolve together. Use `--q` for fills, `--qt` for text on panels, and `--on-q` for text on fills.
- **Do** keep the radius ladder: card 14px, tile 12px, control 10px, strap 8px, pill for chips and tags.
- **Do** paint bar fills on `::before` with `--bar-bg` / `--r`, so the element's own gold focus outline stays visible.
- **Do** set every number in Barlow Condensed with tabular figures.
- **Do** render unknown quality and unknown values as the hatched hollow (a `hatch` border plus the masked 6px diagonal).
- **Do** use `gold-ink`, not `gold`, for gold text and outlines, so the light theme stays AA.
- **Do** keep touch targets at 44px or more, and give small text links an expanded hit area (source marks use 24px).
- **Do** use `hollow` with a 1px `line` border for empty slots and empty states.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gradients, glass (`backdrop-filter`), glow, `text-shadow` or colored shadows.
- **Don't** put the 12° slant on card, tile, button or chip silhouettes. It belongs only to the lookup cap and tally blocks.
- **Don't** use orange, purple or blue for anything but ssr / sr / r quality, and don't recolor them.
- **Don't** use gold for anything other than focus, selection, current or locked.
- **Don't** show unknown quality as gray or as a fourth color, and don't show a guessed number in place of an unknown value.
- **Don't** set blessing or hero names in the display face; it is a closed glyph subset for fixed chrome.
- **Don't** add motion that survives `prefers-reduced-motion`, or motion outside the expo-out 160–260ms grammar.
